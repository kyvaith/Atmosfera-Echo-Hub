# Robot icon sources

The robot-vacuum.svg and robot-mower.svg shapes are Material Design Icons by
Pictogrammers, distributed under Apache 2.0. The upstream notice is retained in
[materialdesignicons-LICENSE.txt](../fonts/materialdesignicons-LICENSE.txt).

Source revision: `2424e748e0cc63ab7b9c095a099b9fe239b737c0`.

- [robot-vacuum.svg](https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign/blob/2424e748e0cc63ab7b9c095a099b9fe239b737c0/svg/robot-vacuum.svg)
- [robot-mower.svg](https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign/blob/2424e748e0cc63ab7b9c095a099b9fe239b737c0/svg/robot-mower.svg)

Product adaptations: H/V commands are explicit L commands, circular arc
segments are cubic curves, and implicitly filled contours are explicitly
closed. Non-rendering root IDs were removed. The single solid fill is tinted
by the native SVG widget; no bitmap or raster source is included.
